3. GW 2
Gut Call Tag — Pattern vs Deep:
Pattern = a generalizable heuristic applied without fixture-specific reasoning (e.g., home fortress form, historical rivalry trends).
Deep = reasoning tied to this specific fixture (team news, current form, conviction-vs-hope splits, tactical matchup).
Tagging rule: when a call mixes both, tag by what's load-bearing — the part actually carrying the uncertainty — not by everything mentioned in the reasoning. A trivial pattern element (e.g., "home teams usually score") doesn't override a fixture-specific judgment call if that's where the real risk sits.
4. Gut Call Notes
"Home nets" — home team scoring is a passive/default assumption, not the emphasized part of the call. Reflects a baseline pattern-read rather than stated conviction.
"Home lock" — home team scoring is explicitly asserted with high conviction, treated as a near-certain anchor point for the rest of the call.
"Away wildcard" — near-certain the away team scores; uncertainty is in how, not whether. Reflects high-confidence BTTS reads (e.g., ≥0.70).
"Away hope" — genuine uncertainty on the away team scoring; conviction is emotional/aspirational rather than evidential. Reflects near-coin-flip BTTS reads (~0.50). The probability, not the note, is the honest signal — the note just names the feeling behind it.
